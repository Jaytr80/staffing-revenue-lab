How to Stop Candidates From Ghosting You (A Practical Playbook for Recruiters)

By Jay Tyler · November 10, 2026

A candidate who disappears before day one costs you a placement, a client's trust and hours of recruiter time. You can't eliminate ghosting, but you can cut it down a lot.

Ghosting usually isn't rude on purpose. The candidate got another offer, got nervous, or simply lost track of you. Most of it happens when communication is thin and expectations are vague. Here's how to tighten both.

Step 1: Set expectations in the first call

Say out loud what happens next: when interviews happen, when decisions come, when start dates are. Then ask: "If something changes or you take another job, will you tell me? I won't be upset, I just need to know so I can help the client."

That one question makes silence harder.

Step 2: Make the job real and specific

Vague jobs get ghosted. Give the candidate the address, shift, pay rate, pay day, dress code, who to ask for and where to park. The more concrete the picture, the more committed they feel.

Step 3: Stay in touch between offer and start

The gap between acceptance and day one is where most candidates drift away. Plan short touches:

  • A same-day text confirming the details
  • A check-in two days before start
  • A reminder the evening before with the exact arrival time
  • A morning-of message asking them to confirm they're on the way

Keep each message short and human. Ask for a reply, not just a read.

Step 4: Ask about the obstacles

Rides, childcare, equipment, background check timing and a competing offer are the common reasons people vanish. Ask directly: "What could get in the way of you starting Monday?" Solve what you can before it becomes a no-show.

Step 5: Always have a backup

Even with perfect follow-up, some people won't show. For high-volume or urgent orders, keep one or two ready candidates warm. Tell the client you're doing it so a no-show doesn't become a missed shift.

Step 6: Track where you lose people

Note when each ghost happened: before interview, after interview, after offer or on start day. Patterns show you where to fix the process, whether it's slow scheduling, unclear pay or weak communication.

Use AI to speed up the follow-up

AI can draft short confirmation texts, interview prep messages and reminders in your tone. Review and personalize each one. The point is faster, clearer contact, not automated spam.

The bottom line

Candidates ghost when they're unsure, uninformed or out of contact. Set expectations early, make the job concrete, stay close through start day and keep a backup. Your fill rate will show it.
